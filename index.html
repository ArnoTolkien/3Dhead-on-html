Head Kit

Put your own 3D head on your Tumblr blog

Upload a head model and its picture. You get a finished Tumblr theme where the head sits in a corner, follows your cursor, and has a little pop-up menu. You don’t need to code anything.

You’ll need a free Tumblr blog, a head model (a .obj file) and the picture that goes with it (.jpg, .png or .bmp).

Step 1: Get your two files

If you have a Blender file, export it like this:

  1. Open the .blend file in Blender (free at blender.org).
  2. Choose File > Export > Wavefront (.obj). Tick Triangulated Mesh, keep Materials ticked, and save.
  3. For the picture, open the Shading tab, click the picture box (it shows the file name), then choose Image > Save As.

Models from other programs work too, as long as they have a UV map. That’s the part that tells the picture where to go on the head.

Step 2: Build your theme

Move your mouse around the preview. If the face looks backwards or sideways, pick a different choice above and press Build my theme again.

Step 3: Put it on Tumblr
  1. On Tumblr, open your blog and click Edit appearance, then Edit theme, then Edit HTML.
  2. Click in the code box, select everything (Ctrl+A, or Cmd+A on a Mac), and delete it.
  3. Paste the theme code you copied in Step 2.
  4. Click Update preview, then Save.
  5. Open your real blog address in a new tab. Tumblr’s preview often ignores mouse movement, so the head only follows your cursor on the live page.
Step 4: Make it yours

Back in Edit appearance, the options on the left let you change things without touching code:

  • Colors for the background, boxes, title bars, text and links.
  • The welcome message, your title and description, and the footer.
  • A background picture (tick Stretch Background Image to fit the screen).
  • Up to six link buttons (88x31 pictures) and three text links.
  • Use 3D Head to turn the head off completely.

Visitors can click the head to hide it or move it to the other corner, and they can bring it back with the Show head button.

If something looks wrong
The face is backwards or sideways

Change Which way does the head face? in Step 2 and build again. One of the four choices will be right.

It says my model has no UV map

The model needs a UV map so the picture can wrap around it. In Blender, tick UV in the export settings, or check that the model has been unwrapped.

The colors look wrong or the head is plain

You may have picked a different picture than the one the model uses. If the model uses several pictures, this kit can only use one, so pick the main face picture.

The page is slow to load

Very detailed models make the page heavier. Simple models with a few hundred triangles work best, and pictures are shrunk to 256 pixels wide automatically.

Can I use heads from a game?

Fan blogs do it all the time, but the models and pictures belong to the people who made them. If you plan to sell anything or run a bigger site, check the rules first.
