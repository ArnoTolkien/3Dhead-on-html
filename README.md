# Head Kit

A little project made by Arno Tolkien in 2024 for his IT project.

Head Kit lets you put an interactive 3D head on your website. You can upload your own 3D model and texture, customize how it looks, and generate a snippet of code to add to your site.

### How to use

1. Upload your `.obj` 3D model.
2. Upload its texture image.
3. Adjust the settings to your liking.
4. Preview your head.
5. Generate and copy the code.
6. Paste the code into your website.

You can customize its position, size, colors, and other settings.
